FocusLens

A little support for your next study session.

Ready when you are.

Make room to focus.

Calibrate your study positions, choose a session, and receive gentle reminders when a distraction lasts.

Your camera footage and images never leave this device. Nothing from your camera is uploaded.

The tracking library and models download over the network. All camera processing happens in your browser. Camera images are never saved. Optional history keeps summary statistics in this browser on this device.

A random local profile ID groups your data; it is not a login. First-distraction timing is saved locally for pacing suggestions, separately from optional summary history.

If you choose Send Report after a session, its summary and text timeline are emailed through your configured sending account. Camera footage is never included.

Phone-free counts a visible phone as distraction after 3 seconds, including an unused phone beside your notes. Studying with phone ignores phone detection. Phones outside camera view cannot be detected.

History is off by default. No footage, head-pose readings, calibration, or event timelines are stored. Clear saved summaries anytime in History.

A visible unused phone counts in phone-free mode. Missing or stale desk-camera readings are ignored; this does not prove the desk is phone-free. Wi-Fi phone-camera support is deferred until USB camera testing passes.

Optional app monitoring · macOS

Reads the frontmost app name only. It never captures screen content, screenshots, browser tabs, or window titles. Enter actual app names; “Chrome” also matches “Google Chrome”. Apps outside your list count as off-task; an empty list allows no apps.

Common distractions include YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Discord, Steam, and games. An allowed browser can still contain distracting tabs; this version cannot distinguish them. Keep FocusLens visible: hiding it still pauses the session.
