Optional desktop app monitoring · macOS

Download the desktop helper and open connect-monitor.command on your Mac. Generate a pairing code below and enter it in the helper. The code expires in two minutes. Pairing lasts up to four hours and can be revoked anytime.

The helper reads app names only while an enabled, unpaused study session has a fresh lease. It cannot read your history or send email, and never reads screenshots, window titles, or browser tabs.
